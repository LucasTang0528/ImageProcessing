PREDICTION - E1.6, green-excluded decay share
Recorded before the arm was run. Nothing below has been revised since.

WHAT THE ARM CHANGES
One dimension of 37. T1_decay_share currently counts any dominant colour
cluster that is dark (CIE L* < 45) and weakly chromatic (chroma < 25).
Shadowed green peel satisfies both, so on unripe fruit the index counts
shade as decay. The arm additionally requires the cluster to be non-green
(a* >= 0). The other 36 dimensions are untouched.

MEASURED EFFECT ON THE DIMENSION ITSELF
Training partition, 590 images, one extractor pass:

  class     n    decay_off  decay_on  removed   green_share
  Unripe  192        10.97      0.69    93.7%         91.99
  Ripe    200         7.75      4.23    45.4%         14.22
  Rotten  198        12.97     12.97     0.0%          6.19

Rotten loses nothing, which is the result the mechanism predicts: real
decay is brown and is never green. Separation of Rotten from Unripe on
this dimension alone goes from 1.18x to 18.81x.

PREDICTED ACCURACY DELTA
  Point estimate  : +0.3 points of cross-validated accuracy
  Interval        : -0.3 to +1.0 points
  Confidence      : low. One dimension in 37 rarely moves an RBF much.

REASONING
The obvious mechanism - better Unripe separation - is predicted NOT to
fire. Unripe is 91.99% green by T1_green_share, a dimension already in
block C, so the classifier can already identify unripe fruit without
reference to the decay index, and can in principle already discount an
inflated decay reading when green_share is high. An RBF kernel on
standardised features is expressive enough to represent that interaction.
The information is therefore probably already present, just not in this
dimension.

The mechanism that could fire is Ripe against Rotten. That is T1's main
error mode - the pilot confusion matrix sends 11.9% of Ripe to Rotten and
15.0% of Rotten to Ripe - and it is the pair least helped by green_share,
since neither class is green. On this dimension the Rotten-to-Ripe ratio
improves from 1.67x to 3.07x, so any real gain should appear there and
almost nowhere else.

WHAT EACH OUTCOME WOULD MEAN
  Gain > +1.0    Unexpected. Would suggest the contaminated dimension was
                 actively misleading the classifier, not merely redundant.
                 Check the per-class table before believing it.
  +0.2 to +1.0   As predicted. Confirm the gain sits in Ripe/Rotten recall
                 and not in Unripe, or the stated mechanism is wrong even
                 though the number agrees.
  -0.3 to +0.2   The likeliest outcome, and a finding rather than a
                 failure: green_share already carries this separation, and
                 the decay index is redundant with it for the purpose the
                 exclusion was meant to serve. Report as redundancy.
  < -0.3         Would mean the shadow contamination was itself useful,
                 presumably as an indirect greenness measure. Report as
                 measured; do not restore the flag default on that basis.

FALSIFIABLE COMMITMENT
If accuracy rises by more than 0.2 points, the per-class table must show
the gain concentrated in Ripe and Rotten. If instead the gain appears in
Unripe, the reasoning above is wrong and must be corrected rather than
retrofitted.
